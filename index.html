Past the Plug

The power and data systems behind modern life, explained.

Past the Plug is a YouTube channel of clear, sourced, animated explainers about what happens past the plug: the electricity grid, the internet's physical layer, the cloud and the supply chains in between.

Watch: youtube.com/@pasttheplug

Contact: pasttheplug@gmail.com

How the videos are made

Every video is researched from primary sources, which are listed in its description. All visuals are original animations drawn for that video. Narration uses a synthetic text-to-speech voice. Every script and every finished video is reviewed by a person before it is published, and corrections are pinned when we get something wrong.

Past the Plug Publisher

Past the Plug Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel's owner to upload and schedule this channel's own videos and to read this channel's own analytics through the YouTube API Services. It has no other users. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses.
